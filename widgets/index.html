Ember widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for your calendar, tasks and weather. Do this on your iPhone.

1
Copy the widget script

Tap the button. It copies the whole script.

Loading…
2
Paste it into Scriptable

Open Scriptable, tap +, paste, then tap the title at the top and rename it Ember Widget. Tap the play button once and enter your Ember password.

3
Add the widget

Long-press your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, find Scriptable and pick a size. Long-press the new widget, tap Edit Widget, set Script to Ember Widget, and set Parameter to today, weather or next.
